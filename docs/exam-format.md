# Exam format

What MockModeler knows about each exam, and where it came from. Checked on 7 November 2026.

Camunda's Academy site refuses automated reading, so these facts come from search excerpts of its
pages, not from the pages or blueprints themselves. Confirm them against the official blueprint, and
update `content/tracks/` to match. The app shows each track's unconfirmed points on its start page.
